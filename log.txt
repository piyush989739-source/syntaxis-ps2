master prompt we used 
Build a production-ready, full-stack Financial Reconciliation web application called "ReconcileIQ" (React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts, Vite, Vitest) designed for the Syntaxis 2026 / Archithon Hackathon (Problem Statement 2: Accounting Automation & Data Reconciliation).

The application must import and reconcile invoices and payments, provide explainable AI suggestions with confidence scores, maintain integer-precision balances, and enforce strict auditability.

---

### 1. CORE FINANCIAL & RECONCILIATION ENGINE (`/src/lib`)

1. **Integer Paise Monetary Math (`money.ts`):**
   - Perform all calculations in integer paise (₹1 = 100 paise) to prevent IEEE-754 floating-point drift.
   - Functions: `toPaise(rupees)`, `fromPaise(paise)`, `formatINR(paise)`, `addPaise()`, `subPaise()`. Zero floating-point rounding errors.

2. **Reference Normalization & Fuzzy Similarity (`normalizer.ts`):**
   - Normalize invoice and payment references: `"INV1001"`, `"inv-1001"`, `"INV 1001"` must all resolve to standard format.
   - Implement Levenshtein distance for fuzzy matching between inconsistent payer/customer names.

3. **CSV Parsing & Validation (`parser.ts`):**
   - Ingest invoice and payment CSV records with validation.
   - Detect and flag invalid records: missing amounts, invalid dates, negative amounts, or duplicate IDs before matching.

4. **Duplicate Detection (`duplicates.ts`):**
   - Detect likely duplicate payments: same customer/payer, identical amount, payment dates within 1 day (±24h), and similar description.
   - Link duplicates together, flag with "Possible Duplicate", and exclude from suggestions by default.
   - Provide two explicit user actions: "Mark as Duplicate" (excludes from balances and logs to report) and "Not a Duplicate" (restores to normal matching).

5. **Partial-Payment & Split Aggregation (`aggregation.ts`):**
   - Split-to-One Match: Search for combinations of 2 to 3 unallocated payments from the same customer whose amounts sum exactly to an invoice's outstanding balance (e.g., `PAY-2009 (₹8,000) + PAY-2010 (₹7,000) = ₹15,000 = INV-1010`).
   - One-to-Multiple Split: Support allocating a single payment across multiple invoices with real-time running total validation.

6. **Explainable Matching Engine (`matcher.ts`):**
   - Multi-factor scoring: Amount match (exact/partial), date proximity (within payment terms), reference matching, and customer name similarity.
   - Output confidence score (0-100%) categorized into High (≥80%), Medium (50-79%), Low (<50%).
   - Cap ambiguous matches (multiple close contenders) at Medium confidence and flag as "Ambiguous".
   - Flag "Amount Mismatch" when refs match but amounts differ.
   - Provide clear textual explanations for each suggestion.

7. **Allocation State & Audit Trail (`allocator.ts`):**
   - Safeguards: A payment cannot be allocated more than once or beyond its available balance. Confirming an over-allocation must be rejected.
   - Maintain ledger balances: customer balances, invoice remaining, payment unallocated.
   - Full audit trail logging every action (confirm, reject, manual assign, undo, mark duplicate) with timestamps and amounts.
   - Support a one-click "Undo" that restores previous balances and suggestions.
