Problem Statement

The Challenge We Solved

Domain: Financial Technology, Accounting Automation & Data Reconciliation

⚠️ The Real-World Problem

Small businesses often maintain invoices and payment records in separate spreadsheets or systems. Differences in customer names, missing invoice references, repeated payment amounts, and unclear transaction descriptions make it difficult to determine which invoices have been paid and which still require attention.

🎯 Core Requirements

  • Import invoice & payment records from CSV files
  • Suggest matches using invoice refs, customer IDs, amounts & dates
  • Provide understandable explanations for every match
  • Allow users to confirm, reject, or correct suggestions
  • Keep ambiguous cases visible for investigation
  • Calculate outstanding balances from confirmed allocations
  • Prevent the same payment from being counted twice

🔬 Extension Features Required

  • Partial-payment aggregation (split matching)
  • Duplicate detection safeguards
  • Fuzzy matching of unclear descriptions
  • Export reconciliation report with decisions
  • Show unmatched invoices & unallocated payments
  • Report: precision, correct-suggestion rate, unresolved count
